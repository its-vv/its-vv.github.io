About

I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Physics at Caltech, working in the group of Prof. Kerry Vahala. My research is in integrated photonics, centered on microresonator frequency combs and their use for precision timing and low-noise microwave generation.

Before Caltech, I received a B.S. in Mathematics and Physics (Honors) from Tsinghua University, and worked on superconductivity in rhombohedral graphene with Prof. Long Ju at MIT.

Research Interests

  • Integrated photonics
  • Microresonator frequency combs
  • Optical frequency division
  • Low-noise microwave synthesis
